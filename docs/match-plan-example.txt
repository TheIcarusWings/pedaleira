# An example plan for 'PedaleiraMatch session' (docs/matching.md).
#
# One take per line: <file stem> | <instruction shown to the player>
# The stem becomes <dir>/<stem>.wav. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.
# Keep every setting that is not named in the instruction where it was in the take before,
# and keep the amp, cab, gate and every other block off or bypassed.
# End a stem with -usb for a take recorded over USB (digital), so that it is never
# compared by mistake with a take that went through the interface converters.

# The unit's own path, the first thing to compare against when something looks off.
bypass | All blocks bypassed, input and output levels at unity

# A delay at three times, feedback 35%, mix 100% wet.
delay-analog-250ms-fb35-wet | Delay (analog): time 250 ms, feedback 35%, mix 100%
delay-analog-500ms-fb35-wet | Delay (analog): time 500 ms, feedback 35%, mix 100%
delay-analog-750ms-fb35-wet | Delay (analog): time 750 ms, feedback 35%, mix 100%

# A compressor at two ratios, threshold -30 dB.
comp-ratio4-thr-30 | Compressor: ratio 4:1, threshold -30 dB, attack and release at their defaults, makeup 0 dB
comp-ratio10-thr-30 | Compressor: ratio 10:1, threshold -30 dB, attack and release at their defaults, makeup 0 dB

# A reverb at two decay times, mix 100% wet.
reverb-hall-decay2-wet | Reverb (hall): decay 2 s, mix 100%
reverb-hall-decay3-wet | Reverb (hall): decay 3 s, mix 100%
