How it works

Each round plays a real recording, the Reference, and several numbered clips. Some clips are recreations, one may be the reference itself, and one is deliberately worse. Score each clip from 0 to 100 for how close it sounds to the reference: 100 means you cannot tell them apart.

Use headphones or good speakers, at a comfortable level you keep the same for the whole test. Switching clips keeps the playback position, so you can compare the same phrase. Keys: R reference, 1 to 6 clips, Space play or stop.
